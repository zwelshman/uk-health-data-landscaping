# Making Secondary Care Medicines Data Research-Ready: Project Analysis & BHF DSC Support Strategy

## Executive Summary

The **"Making secondary care medicines data research-ready"** project is an HDRS Technical Transformation Programme initiative launching **October 2026**, led by **NHS England SDE** with partners including the **Professional Record Standards Body (PRSB)**, **University of Liverpool**, **Understanding Patient Data**, and **King's College Hospital NHS Foundation Trust**.

The project aims to develop **standards and tooling** to make **hospital prescribing and high-cost drugs data research-ready** across UK Secure Data Environments (SDEs).

## How BHF DSC Services Can Drive Project Success

Based on the BHF DSC Services Reference, here are **7 critical service areas** that directly align with and can accelerate this project:

### 1. **Data Curation & Engineering Services** ⭐ *Highest Impact*
- **Feasibility assessment** for medicines data across all 4 UK SDEs
- **Computable phenotype implementation** for medicine exposure/outcome definitions
- **Cohort, exposure, outcome, covariate creation** tailored to medicines research
- **Documented, version-controlled, repeatable curation pipelines** - exactly what the project needs
- **Cross-nation adaptation** for England/Scotland/Wales governance differences

### 2. **Computable Phenotypes & Codelists** ⭐ *Highest Impact*
- **HDR UK Phenotype Library** with BHF DSC-contributed cardiovascular medicine phenotypes
- **Codelist Comparison Tool** - maps dm+d/SNOMED/BNF codes, compares with GDPPR reference, shows SDE counts
- **Pre-validated CVD medicine phenotypes**: anticoagulants, antiplatelets, statins, ACE inhibitors, beta-blockers, SGLT2 inhibitors, ARNI
- **Terminology mapping tables** for medicines code translation
- **FAIR sharing guidance** for phenotype definitions

### 3. **Curated Assets** ⭐ *High Impact*
- **Harmonised HES APC diagnoses & procedures** - directly relevant for secondary care context
- **Standardised HES APC procedure preparation** - for procedure-linked medicines
- **Harmonised mortality variables** - critical for medicine outcome studies
- **Reusable code components** with documentation for adaptation

### 4. **Population Data Dashboard** ⭐ *High Impact*
- **Cross-nation coverage view** across NHS England SDE, Scotland Safe Haven, SAIL Wales
- **Dataset completeness, linkage opportunities** for medicines datasets
- **Geographical scope, time periods, coding systems** metadata
- **Record-volume summaries** for feasibility planning

### 5. **UK CliC TRE Infrastructure** ⭐ *Medium-High Impact*
- **Consented cohort linkage** to routine NHS medicines data
- **Secure analysis environment** for linked cohort-medicines studies
- **SAIL Databank approval process** for controlled access
- **Support for clinical, biomarker, imaging, omics data** linkage

### 6. **Patient & Public Involvement (PPIE)** ⭐ *Medium Impact*
- **Co-design inclusive PPIE** for medicines data research
- **Connect with lived-experience groups** for medication safety/effectiveness
- **Plain English summaries** for public-facing materials
- **Public Advisory Group** for review and partnership

### 7. **Open & Reproducible Research Practices** ⭐ *Medium Impact*
- **GitHub organisation** for code sharing and collaboration
- **HDR UK Phenotype Library** integration
- **Documented processing decisions** for transparency
- **FAIR-sharing guidance** for research assets

- **Domain expertise** (cardiovascular medicines, HES curation)
- **Governance framework** (PPIE, IG compliance, FAIR principles)

...directly addresses the project's core challenges. **Early, deep integration** with BHF DSC services should be a project priority, not an optional add-on.
